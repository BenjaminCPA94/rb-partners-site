FAQ

Questions foreign companies ask before choosing a French accountant.

Can you work directly with our CFO or finance team outside France?

Yes. Communication can be organised in English, and the French closing and reporting process can be aligned with your head-office calendar where required.

Can you take over an existing French subsidiary?

Yes. We can organise the handover of accounting records, tax filings, access rights and open items, then establish the recurring French workflow with your teams.

Can you handle both French accounting and payroll?

Yes, depending on the agreed engagement scope. Coordinating the two can simplify information flows and recurring deadlines.

Do you support a French branch as well as a subsidiary?

Yes. The accounting and compliance setup depends on the form of French presence and the activities carried out locally, so we first review the intended operating model.

Can you provide reporting in English?

Yes. Periodic reporting and communication can be organised in English, with formats adapted to the information expected by headquarters.
