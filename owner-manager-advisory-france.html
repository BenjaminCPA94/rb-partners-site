FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you tell me whether salary or dividends are better?

We can compare the options, but the answer depends on your current compensation, social status, available cash, company plans and personal situation. We quantify the relevant scenarios before reviewing them with you. There is no single answer for every owner-manager.

Do you support holding-company setups?

We support the economic, accounting and tax thinking around a holding company and its role in the group. Incorporation and legal formalities are handled through our Legal & company formation service. Complex legal steps are coordinated with lawyers or notaries.

Can you work with my lawyer or wealth adviser?

Yes. Coordination is particularly useful for sales, succession, structuring and personal-tax matters. We share relevant assumptions with the advisers involved within the framework agreed with you.

Can you support me before selling my business?

Yes. We can prepare scenarios around ownership structure, expected flows and the accounting and tax consequences to review. Sale documentation, legal work and specialist wealth matters are coordinated with the relevant advisers.

Do you support non-resident or inbound owner-managers?

Yes, for French matters within our scope. The position needs to be reviewed in light of tax residence, compensation, the French company and any applicable international treaties. Matters outside France are coordinated with the relevant advisers.
