FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need monthly accounting first?

Not necessarily. However, the frequency and quality of available data determine the level of management reporting that is possible. We therefore review accounting flows and production timing before building the indicators.

Can you take over an existing group reporting pack?

Yes. We can work from the template, chart of accounts and calendar provided by headquarters. We identify the French data to produce and any necessary adjustments. Variance comments can be prepared in English.

Do you prepare one-off forecasts?

Yes. A forecast can support a hiring decision, investment, new activity or another specific project. We define assumptions with you and show the impact on profit, working capital and cash.

Can you prepare a forecast for a bank?

Yes, where this falls within our engagement. We build assumptions from your activity, accounts and financing project. The resulting document can support discussions with the bank.

Can you work directly with the group finance team?

Yes. We can coordinate with the group finance team or controller. This helps align formats, reporting dates and the explanations expected for variances.
