FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you handle our recurring French tax filings?

We handle the filings included in the engagement letter and clearly define the calendar and responsibilities.

Do you support companies with cross-border flows?

Yes. We can handle the French side of cross-border transactions and coordinate with foreign advisers where their input is needed.

Can you take over a file with late filings or open tax matters?

Yes. We first identify the periods concerned, filings already submitted, payments made and missing information in order to prioritise corrective work.

What happens if the French tax authorities contact us?

We organise the available information, review the request within our engagement scope and prepare the required information with you. Disputes or legal matters can be coordinated with a tax lawyer.

Can you work directly with our finance team or headquarters?

Yes. We can work with French or foreign finance teams and align reporting with the group calendar.
