FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you manage payroll for a foreign-owned French entity?

Yes. We can set up and manage French payroll for a subsidiary or local team. Communication with headquarters can be in English. International mobility or employment-law matters requiring specialist advice are coordinated with the relevant advisers.

Can you take over payroll during the year?

Yes. We take over employee data, payroll totals, settings and available filings. A takeover review identifies any items that need correction or completion before the next cycle.

Do you handle employment contracts?

We can prepare routine documents within our engagement and the information required for hiring. Where a clause or situation requires specialist employment-law advice, we work with the company’s lawyer. The scope is agreed at the outset.

How do you determine the applicable collective agreement?

We review the company’s activity, organisation and available information. Where identification requires specific legal analysis, we recommend validation with specialist counsel. The selected agreement then affects several payroll settings.

How do we send payroll variables?

The workflow is defined when the payroll file is set up. Variables can be centralised in the chosen collaborative tool or through a format agreed with your team. The objective is to maintain one identified source and a stable calendar.
