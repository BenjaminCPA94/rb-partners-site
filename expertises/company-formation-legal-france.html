FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can a French company be incorporated fully remotely?

A large part of the process can be organised remotely. Feasibility still depends on the shareholders, documents to be signed and the specific formalities required. We identify any additional steps and documentation at the start.

Do you support holding companies?

Yes, for formation and recurring corporate work within our scope. We review the holding company’s place in the group and coordinate the necessary formalities. Personal owner-manager matters are handled through our Owner-manager advisory service and complex operations with specialist advisers.

Can you take over annual corporate work for an existing company?

Yes. We review articles, recent minutes, registers and other useful historical information. Future annual approvals and filings are then integrated into the engagement calendar.

What documents are required for a foreign shareholder or director?

Requirements depend on the person’s status, country of residence and the entity being created. We prepare a file-specific document list and flag any translation, legalisation or additional formalities that may be required.

Can you help us choose between a subsidiary and a branch?

Yes, within our scope and in coordination with your other advisers where needed. The choice depends on the desired autonomy, group organisation and accounting, tax and legal consequences. We review these factors before formalities begin.
