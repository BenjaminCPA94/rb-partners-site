FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you take over during the financial year?

Yes. We can take over the accounting without waiting for the next year-end. We organise the transfer of trial balances, ledgers, FEC files, tax filings and other relevant information. We then review entries to be addressed, upcoming deadlines and any open items.

Do you work with Pennylane?

Yes. Pennylane can centralise invoices, bank flows, supporting documents and accounting-related exchanges. We organise the file setup and approval workflows with your team. This gives each stakeholder a clear place to submit, approve and retrieve information.

Can you report to a foreign parent company?

Yes. We can prepare periodic reports in French or English using the group’s required format. This may include chart-of-accounts mapping, intercompany reconciliations, group-GAAP adjustments and consolidation packages. The reporting calendar is built around the parent company’s closing deadlines.

Can you prepare interim accounts during the year?

Yes. Interim accounts can be prepared monthly, quarterly or at a specific date depending on your needs. They include a review of the main accounts and the entries required for the period. The level of detail is defined according to how the information will be used.

How do you handle French e-invoicing?

We integrate the e-invoicing reform into the accounting setup. We review your current invoice issuing, receipt and approval workflows and adapt them to the new requirements. When Pennylane is used, we make sure the invoice workflow remains consistent with the rest of the accounting process.
