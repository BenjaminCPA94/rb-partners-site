FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you help us choose a platform?

Yes. We start from your volumes, flows, organisation and existing tools to define requirements. We can then help select an appropriate accredited platform and organise its setup. We do not recommend adding tools without an operational reason.

Do we need to change accounting software?

Not necessarily. The reform mainly requires the relevant flows to pass through an accredited platform. We therefore review the compatibility of your current setup, including Pennylane where you use it, before proposing changes.

Can you support several companies in one group?

Yes. We can map the flows of several entities and identify common and entity-specific rules. This is particularly useful for intercompany processes, local responsibilities and international transactions.

Are we affected if we only sell to consumers?

B2C sales do not follow the same B2B e-invoicing flow, but they can fall within e-reporting. Your business is also affected by the requirement to receive electronic supplier invoices. We review the flows to determine which obligations apply.

What changes for supplier invoices?

Since 1 September 2026, affected businesses must be able to receive electronic invoices through an accredited platform. Receipt, approval and accounting integration therefore need to be organised around that platform. We also review who approves invoices and how supporting documents reach accounting.
