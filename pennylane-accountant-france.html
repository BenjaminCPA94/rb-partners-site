Our approach

Make Pennylane work for your organisation

We start by understanding your workflows: purchases, sales, banking, expenses, invoicing and reporting needs. The aim is to avoid tool overload and create a simple process.

We then configure the everyday uses that matter: document collection, bank feeds, invoicing, document tracking and information sharing with the firm.

Pennylane fits into a broader finance setup including accounting, management reporting, e-invoicing and, where useful, additional automation.

FAQ

Questions before choosing your accountant.

Do you use Pennylane?

Yes. Pennylane is one of the tools we can use depending on the file and client organisation.

Can you take over an existing Pennylane file?

Yes. We can take over the existing environment and review settings, access rights and workflows.

Does Pennylane replace the accountant?

No. It helps automate and organise flows; review, filings, closing and advisory remain professional work.

Can you explain the workflow to our team?

Yes. We define roles and explain the selected upload, approval and follow-up process.
