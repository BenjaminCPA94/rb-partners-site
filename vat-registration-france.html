FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does my foreign company have to register for VAT in France?

It depends on your activity: distance selling, services, stock held in France or a local subsidiary can each trigger a different obligation. We review your situation before any filing.

Do I need a French subsidiary to register for VAT?

No. A foreign company can register for VAT in France without setting up a subsidiary, typically through the French tax office for foreign businesses.

What is a fiscal representative, and when is one required?

A fiscal representative is a person or entity that answers for a foreign company's French VAT obligations. It is required in certain cases depending on the company's home country, notably outside the European Union.

Can you handle our VAT returns on an ongoing basis, not just the registration?

Yes. We handle the registration and then the ongoing returns, coordinated with your accounting team or headquarters abroad.
