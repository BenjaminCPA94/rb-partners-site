Switching accountants in France

Change your French accountant without a gap in your books.

A French company can change accounting firm under the terms of its engagement letter (lettre de mission). Meyvin runs the handover in English: contact with your current firm, transfer of the accounting history and continuity of French filings.

Discuss your switch

Before you switch

Three things to check before writing to your current firm

01

Your engagement letter

The lettre de mission sets the term, the notice period and how termination must be notified. It, rather than any general rule, decides when the change can take effect.

02

Timing within the financial year

Switching right after year-end closing is the simplest, but a mid-year change is possible as long as it is clear which firm files which return.

03

What belongs to you

Your source documents, accounting entries (the FEC file), tax returns and payslips should be transferable to the new firm. List them before the transition.

Typical situations

Why international teams change French accountant

01

Head office cannot get answers

Replies are slow or only in French, and your finance team hears about French issues after the deadline.

02

Reporting does not match group needs

03

Our handover method

A transition organised in four steps

We start with a call with a partner to understand the French entity, read the current engagement letter and set a realistic switch date around the notice period and upcoming deadlines.

Once the new engagement letter is signed, we write to your previous accountant, as French professional rules require, and agree the transfer of the file with them.

We collect the accounting history, latest tax returns and, where relevant, payroll data, then set up your tools — bank feeds, invoicing, document collection — so production resumes without a gap.

You then receive a calendar of upcoming French deadlines and a first status review, in English, that your head office can rely on.

FAQ

Switching accountants in France: your questions

Can a company change accountant mid-year in France?

Yes. A change can take place during the financial year, subject to the notice period and terms of your engagement letter. We then agree with you, and with the previous firm, who prepares each filing during the transition.

What is the notice period to leave a French accountant?

There is no single notice period: it is set by your engagement letter. Check the term and termination clause, or send us the document and we will tell you when the change can take effect.

Does the previous firm have to hand over our file?

Documents that belong to the company and the information needed to continue the engagement should be transferable to the new firm. In practice we contact the previous accountant directly to organise it. Settling outstanding fees beforehand avoids the most common blocking point.

Can the whole process be handled in English?

Yes. Calls, emails and status reviews can be in English, and we deal with the previous firm and the French authorities in French on your behalf.

Will we have to change software?

Not necessarily. We work notably with Pennylane for accounting and Silae for payroll, and we start from your existing tools: the aim is simpler flows, not an unnecessary migration.
