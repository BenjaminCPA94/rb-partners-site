Mandatory e-invoicing : what's changing for your business

September 2026 6 min read

Since 1 September 2026, affected businesses must be able to receive electronic invoices through an accredited platform. Issuance is already mandatory for large companies and mid-caps; affected SMEs and micro-businesses must issue by 1 September 2027 at the latest.

Why this reform?

The tax authorities have three stated goals : fighting VAT fraud more effectively, eventually simplifying businesses' reporting obligations, and giving the State near real-time visibility into economic activity. France is aligning with several European neighbors that have already rolled out mandatory e-invoicing.

Who's affected, and from when?

Since 1 September 2026, affected businesses must be able to receive electronic invoices. Large companies and mid-caps have also been required to issue them since that date. For affected SMEs and micro-businesses, the issuance deadline is 1 September 2027 at the latest.

Please note, The timetable is now operational: receipt from 1 September 2026 for affected businesses; issuance from that date for large companies and mid-caps, then by 1 September 2027 at the latest for affected SMEs and micro-businesses.

Source officielle : DGFiP, Facturation électronique et plateformes agréées ↗

Two obligations not to confuse

  • E-invoicing : issuing and receiving structured electronic invoices, transmitted through an approved platform, for your domestic B2B transactions.
  • E-reporting : sending the tax authorities transaction data not covered by e-invoicing (sales to consumers, international transactions), as well as payment data.

How to prepare now

  • Choose an accredited platform suited to your flows and operating model
  • Check that your invoicing or accounting software is compatible with the required format
  • Train the teams who issue or process invoices on the new workflows
  • Plan ahead for the legally compliant electronic archiving of your invoices

This reform is significant, but it can be prepared for calmly with a bit of lead time. Our teams follow the regulatory news closely and can help you choose your solution and secure your transition.
