Starting a business in France : the 5 key steps

June 2026 5 min read

Starting out is an exciting adventure, but it benefits from structure. Here are the five steps we see come up, project after project, with the entrepreneurs we support.

1. Validate and cost out your project

Before any administrative steps, take the time to formalize your project : who you're serving, how you'll generate revenue, and above all, what financial resources you're committing at launch. Even a simple financial forecast saves you many unpleasant surprises.

2. Choose the right legal structure

Sole proprietorship, micro-business, single-shareholder company, multi-shareholder company : each form carries its own tax, social protection and governance rules. This choice depends on your activity, whether you have co-founders, and your growth plans, it deserves guidance, not a solo decision in front of an online form.

3. Draft the bylaws and raise the capital

If you're forming a company, the bylaws formalize how it operates : capital allocation, the manager's powers, decision-making rules. It's also the time to open a dedicated bank account and deposit the share capital.

4. Register your company

Registering with the trade and companies register legally brings your business into existence and lets you obtain your official registration certificate. The formalities are now largely handled online, but care is still needed with the supporting documents requested.

5. Set up your management tools from day one

Accounting, invoicing, cash flow tracking : the earlier these tools are in place, the easier it is to steer your business in the first months. It's also the ideal time to agree with your accountant on how often you'll check in.

Every project is different, and these steps never quite unfold in the same order. We support founders from their earliest thinking, to secure the choices that matter.
