How we work

Software for production, partners for decisions

Software does what it does best: pulling bank feeds, reading invoices, preparing entries and keeping a dashboard up to date. We work notably with Pennylane for accounting and Silae for payroll.

The firm does the rest: reviewing the books, preparing French tax returns and annual accounts, warning you before a deadline and helping you weigh decisions on pay, hiring, investment or structure.

In France, keeping a company's accounts on its behalf is reserved to chartered accountants (experts-comptables). Online or not, your accounts are prepared under the responsibility of the firm's two partners.

FAQ

Online accountant in France: your questions

Is an online accountant in France a regulated accountant?

It should be: keeping accounts for third parties is a regulated activity in France. At Meyvin, “online” describes how we work — connected tools and remote meetings — not a lighter service: your accounts are followed by the firm's partners.

Do I need to travel to Paris?

No. Meetings can be held by video and all documents are exchanged online. Meeting in Paris remains possible if you prefer.

Can everything be done in English?

Yes. Calls, emails and reporting can be in English. Statutory filings are made in French, and we explain what they contain.

Which tools do you use?

Mainly Pennylane for accounting, invoicing and cash monitoring, and Silae for payroll. If you already use other tools, we first look at how to keep them.

How much does an online accountant cost in France?

Fees depend on transaction volume, headcount and the scope entrusted to the firm. They are agreed in advance in an engagement letter after a first conversation.
