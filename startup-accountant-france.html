How we help

Support that evolves with every stage of growth

At launch, we secure the structure, accounting and tax obligations and the right tools. The goal is to avoid manual rework and build a clean finance foundation from day one.

As you grow, we strengthen cash tracking, margins, budgets, reporting and the financial data needed for investors, banks and other stakeholders.

We can also coordinate payroll, tax, annual legal work and group structuring so you retain one central point of contact as the business becomes more complex.

Typical assignments

  • Bookkeeping & annual accounts
  • Cash monitoring, burn rate & runway
  • Budgets, KPIs & investor reporting
  • Financial data-room preparation
  • Business plans & forecasts
  • Payroll, hiring & BSPCE
  • R&D tax incentives where eligible
  • Holding and growth structuring
