FAQ

Questions foreign companies ask before operating in France.

Do we need to create a French subsidiary?

Not necessarily. The appropriate setup depends on the activity, intended presence, level of autonomy and the legal, tax and payroll consequences. We help frame the French operating model and coordinate with legal advisers where required.

Can you take over an existing French subsidiary?

Yes. We can organise the accounting, tax and payroll handover, review upcoming deadlines and align reporting with your finance team or headquarters.

Can you work directly with our CFO or finance team abroad?

Yes. The international service is designed around direct communication with headquarters, in English or French, with reporting adapted to the group calendar.

Can you manage both accounting and payroll in France?

Yes, depending on the agreed scope. Coordinating the two helps avoid fragmented information flows between finance and payroll.

Can you support a company before it has a full finance team in France?

Yes. We can provide the French accounting and compliance backbone while your local organisation is still being built, then adapt the scope as internal resources grow.
