Worked example

How we support a French subsidiary, month after month.

This example illustrates our method. It does not describe a real client, and it commits us to no turnaround time or calendar: those are agreed with each group.

01

Collecting the documents

The subsidiary drops its purchase and sales invoices and its bank statements into a shared online tool such as Pennylane. Missing documents are flagged as they arise rather than at year-end.

02

Keeping the local books

Entries are posted and reconciled to the bank under French accounting rules. VAT, payroll and local tax obligations follow the French calendar.

03

Closing

Review of the accounts, closing entries, reconciliation of intercompany balances, then preparation of the French annual accounts.

04

English reporting for head office

The French accounts are presented in the format the group asks for, with an English commentary on significant variances and points of attention.

What head office receives, for example: periodic reporting in its own format, a commented list of variances, the status of intercompany balances and the calendar of French deadlines. The exact content depends on what the group requires.

FAQ

Questions foreign companies ask before operating in France.

Do we need to create a French subsidiary?

Not necessarily. The appropriate setup depends on the activity, intended presence, level of autonomy and the legal, tax and payroll consequences. We help frame the French operating model and coordinate with legal advisers where required.

Can you take over an existing French subsidiary?

Yes. We can organise the accounting, tax and payroll handover, review upcoming deadlines and align reporting with your finance team or headquarters.

Can you work directly with our CFO or finance team abroad?

Yes. The international service is designed around direct communication with headquarters, in English or French, with reporting adapted to the group calendar.

Can you manage both accounting and payroll in France?

Yes, depending on the agreed scope. Coordinating the two helps avoid fragmented information flows between finance and payroll.

Can you support a company before it has a full finance team in France?

Yes. We can provide the French accounting and compliance backbone while your local organisation is still being built, then adapt the scope as internal resources grow.
