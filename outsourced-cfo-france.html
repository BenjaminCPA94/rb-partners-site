How we help

On-demand financial leadership, run by your accounting team

Our outsourced CFO service builds on your existing accounting and tools to produce monthly or quarterly reporting that is genuinely useful for decisions: cash position, margin, profitability by activity and commitments to monitor.

We build forecasts and budgets with you and keep them updated as the business evolves, so hiring, investment and financing decisions rely on current numbers rather than last year's accounts.

For structuring events — fundraising, a sale, external growth, international expansion — we prepare the financial information expected by investors, banks or buyers, coordinated with your other advisers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between an outsourced CFO and an accountant?

Your accountant produces the accounts and meets statutory obligations. The outsourced CFO service uses that accounting data to build reporting, forecasts and financial recommendations. At RB Partners, both roles are coordinated by the same team.

At what stage does an outsourced CFO make sense?

As soon as you need regular reporting, reliable forecasts or preparation for a transaction (fundraising, a sale), without enough volume to justify a full-time hire.

Can you support us ahead of a fundraising round or a sale?

Yes. We prepare the expected financial information (reporting, forecasts, data room) alongside your lawyers and any M&A advisers.

Do you work with groups that have several affiliates, including abroad?

Yes. We coordinate consolidated reporting and financial communication between French subsidiaries and headquarters or international teams.
